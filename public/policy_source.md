# Northstar Consulting Teaching Expense Policy

Version 1.0 · effective 2026-01-01 · Fictional teaching policy authored for this prototype. Not a real employer policy or a legal/tax rule. All sections below are the actual local sources cited by policy cards.

## DOC-001 Receipt evidence

Each claim needs a readable itemized receipt identifying merchant, date, total and currency. Missing required evidence requires human review; a missing receipt number alone is not disqualifying.

Exceptions: None

## GEN-002 Submission deadline

Submit within 30 calendar days after the receipt date, inclusive. Later submissions are likely ineligible when dates are established. A submission before the expense date or an unknown date requires human review.

Exceptions: None

## GEN-003 Duplicate claims

A confirmed duplicate claim is likely ineligible. A clear historical duplicate check is required before likely eligible; an unknown check requires human review. Receipt ID alone does not prove duplication.

Exceptions: None

## GEN-004 Business purpose and approval

Every claim requires a stated business purpose and documented manager approval before a likely-eligible pre-screen. Missing approval requires human review. All pre-screen results await a final Finance decision; no model can approve payment.

Exceptions: None

## MEAL-001 Meal limit

Business meals are limited to SGD 50 per attendee per meal, inclusive of taxes and service charges. The supported attendee count must be known. A documented amount above the per-person limit is likely ineligible.

Exceptions: None

## MEAL-002 Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable. If alcohol is included in the claimed amount, that submitted claim is likely ineligible. If allocation is unclear, human review is required; a corrected claim excluding alcohol is assessed separately.

Exceptions: None

## MEAL-003 Client meals

For client meals, attendee names, organizations and business purpose must be documented. Missing details require human review. For non-client meals this card adds no further requirement.

Exceptions: None

## TRANSPORT-001 Public transport

Actual public-transport business-trip fares are reimbursable when business purpose and route evidence are documented. Personal commuting is not reimbursable.

Exceptions: None

## TRANSPORT-002 Taxi and ride hail

Business taxi or ride-hail fares are limited to SGD 80 per trip. Above that limit is likely ineligible except where TRANSPORT-003 applies. Route and business purpose must be supported.

Exceptions: Late-night business trips are governed by TRANSPORT-003.

## TRANSPORT-003 Late-night travel

For documented business travel between 22:00 and 06:00 local time, taxi or ride-hail fares up to SGD 120 per trip may be pre-screened eligible with documented manager approval and route/time evidence. Above SGD 120 is likely ineligible. Unknown time or approval requires human review.

Exceptions: Replaces the SGD 80 taxi cap only when all night-travel conditions are evidenced.

## TRANSPORT-004 Route evidence

All transport claims require journey date, origin, destination and business purpose evidence. Unknown transport mode or missing route evidence requires human review.

Exceptions: None

## HOTEL-001 Hotel limit

Accommodation room charges including taxes are capped at SGD 250 per night. The number of nights and room-charge allocation must be supported. Amount above the cap is assessed under HOTEL-003; an unresolved allocation requires human review.

Exceptions: HOTEL-003 governs over-limit cases.

## HOTEL-002 Personal hotel items

Personal hotel items such as minibar, spa or leisure charges are excluded. A submitted claim including established personal charges is likely ineligible; an unclear split requires human review.

Exceptions: None

## HOTEL-003 Over-limit accommodation

Over-limit accommodation always requires Finance human review to verify an exception. Attach prior approval if it exists; if none exists, request a Finance exception review. An employee statement or manager confirmation is not by itself a final approval.

Exceptions: No automatic eligibility for over-limit stays, even with prior approval.

## AIR-001 Flight class

Business air travel is limited to economy class. A clearly identified higher class is likely ineligible; an unknown class requires human review.

Exceptions: None

## AIR-002 Travel preapproval

Business flight claims require documented approval obtained before travel. Absent or unverified preapproval requires human review.

Exceptions: None

## FX-001 Foreign currency

Non-SGD claims require a documented conversion rate and its supporting bank or payment record. Convert the relevant amount into SGD before comparing caps; do not use a live or guessed exchange rate. Missing conversion evidence requires human review.

Exceptions: None

## LOST-001 Lost receipt

A lost-receipt declaration and alternative payment evidence may be submitted, but every lost-receipt claim requires human review. The declaration never makes a claim automatically eligible.

Exceptions: None
