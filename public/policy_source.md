# Northstar Consulting Teaching Expense Policy

Version 2.0 · effective 2026-01-01 · Fictional teaching policy authored for this prototype. Not a real employer policy or a legal/tax rule. All sections below are the actual local sources cited by policy cards.

Every rule below states a requirement and a binary outcome. Whether a claim carries enough information to be assessed at all is decided before this policy is applied, by the intake validation layer; this policy does not describe system behaviour.

## DOC-001 Receipt evidence

Each claim must include a readable itemized receipt identifying merchant, date, total and currency. A claim without an itemized receipt does not pass. A missing receipt number alone is not disqualifying.

Exceptions: None

## GEN-002 Submission deadline

A claim must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the receipt date, inclusive. A claim submitted after that window does not pass. A claim submitted before the expense date does not pass.

Exceptions: None

## GEN-003 Duplicate claims

A claim that a historical duplicate check identifies as already reimbursed does not pass. A receipt ID alone does not establish duplication.

Exceptions: None

## GEN-004 Business purpose and approval

Every claim must state a business purpose and carry documented manager approval. A claim missing either does not pass. No pre-screening result is a payment approval; Finance makes the final reimbursement decision.

Exceptions: None

## MEAL-001 Meal limit

Business meals are limited to SGD 50 per attendee per meal, inclusive of taxes and service charges. A claim above the per-attendee limit does not pass.

Exceptions: None

## MEAL-002 Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable. A claim whose amount includes alcohol does not pass. A corrected claim that excludes the alcohol portion is assessed separately.

Exceptions: None

## MEAL-003 Client meals

A client meal must document attendee names, attendee organizations and the business purpose. A client meal without these details does not pass. For a non-client meal this rule adds no further requirement.

Exceptions: None

## TRANSPORT-001 Public transport

Actual public-transport fares for business travel pass this rule when the business purpose and route evidence are documented. Personal commuting does not pass.

Exceptions: None

## TRANSPORT-002 Taxi and ride hail

Business taxi or ride-hail fares are limited to SGD 80 per trip. A fare above that limit does not pass, except where TRANSPORT-003 applies.

Exceptions: Late-night business trips are governed by TRANSPORT-003.

## TRANSPORT-003 Late-night travel

For business travel documented between 22:00 and 06:00 local time, taxi or ride-hail fares up to SGD 120 per trip pass this rule when manager approval and route and time evidence are documented. A fare above SGD 120 does not pass.

Exceptions: Replaces the SGD 80 taxi cap only when all night-travel conditions are evidenced.

## TRANSPORT-004 Route evidence

Every transport claim must document the journey date, origin, destination and business purpose. A transport claim without route evidence does not pass.

Exceptions: None

## HOTEL-001 Hotel limit

Accommodation room charges including taxes are capped at SGD 250 per night. A claim at or below the cap for the documented number of nights passes this rule. A claim above the cap is assessed under HOTEL-003.

Exceptions: HOTEL-003 governs over-limit cases.

## HOTEL-002 Personal hotel items

Personal hotel items such as minibar, spa or leisure charges are not reimbursable. A claim whose amount includes personal charges does not pass. A corrected claim covering only the room charge is assessed separately.

Exceptions: None

## HOTEL-003 Over-limit accommodation

Accommodation above the SGD 250 nightly cap is not reimbursable unless documented prior approval for the higher rate is attached to the claim. With documented prior approval the claim passes this rule; without it the claim does not pass. Convenience alone is not a justification, and an employee statement is not prior approval.

Exceptions: None

## AIR-001 Flight class

Business air travel is limited to economy class. A claim for a higher class does not pass.

Exceptions: None

## AIR-002 Travel preapproval

A business flight claim must carry documented approval obtained before travel. A flight claim without documented pre-approval does not pass.

Exceptions: None

## FX-001 Foreign currency

A non-SGD claim must include a documented conversion rate and the supporting bank or payment record. The documented rate is used to convert the amount into SGD before comparing it against any cap. A live or estimated rate is never used.

Exceptions: None

## LOST-001 Lost receipt

A lost-receipt claim must include a signed lost-receipt declaration and alternative payment evidence, such as a bank or card statement showing the transaction. With both, the claim is assessed under the normal category rules for its expense type. Without both, the claim does not pass.

Exceptions: None
